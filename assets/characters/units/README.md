# Combat unit portraits

Six original techno-fantasy portraits used by the garrison cards and battle reports:

- `coast_guard.webp` — agile coastal scout with a crystal bow.
- `rune_spearman.webp` — marine polearm fighter with a rune core.
- `stone_warden.webp` — articulated heavy exo-armor and energy shield.
- `wind_rider.webp` — glider-equipped aerial scout.
- `crystal_mortar.webp` — crew member beside a compact arcane siege device.
- `storm_drake.webp` — small bioluminescent flying creature.

The supplied recruitment and battle-result images were used to guide layout and framing. Their Napoleonic-looking character designs are not used for these portraits.
